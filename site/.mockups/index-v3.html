03 // Process

No big bangs. Only rollbacks.

STEP 01

Discover

Two weeks of audits, profiling and honest conversations. You get a written map of what's broken — and why it matters to the P&L.

STEP 02

Architect

Reference architecture, cost model, risk register and a migration plan with a rollback path at every single step.

STEP 03

Build & ship

Small batches, demoed weekly. Production traffic is switched over gradually — never in one heroic weekend.

STEP 04

Operate

We stay on the pager, or train your team to be. SLOs, dashboards and quarterly chaos drills keep it honest.
